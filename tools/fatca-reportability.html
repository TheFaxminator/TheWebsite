ACCOUNT REPORTABILITY

Is the account reportable?

A Reporting FI must report its US Reportable Accounts — those held by one or more Specified US Persons, or by a Passive NFFE with one or more Controlling Persons who are Specified US Persons. This tool works through an individual or entity account to reach a reportability outcome. It assumes you have already established that the holder is at a Reporting or Participating FFI.

Who is NOT a Specified US Person

A US person is a Specified US Person unless it falls within one of the excepted categories below. An account held by an excepted US person is generally not reportable.

A corporation whose stock is regularly traded on an established securities market, and its affiliates
The United States or any of its wholly owned agencies or instrumentalities
Any State, US territory, political subdivision or wholly owned agency thereof
A bank, REIT, regulated investment company or registered dealer in securities/commodities
A tax-exempt organisation (501(a)), individual retirement plan, common trust fund or exempt trust

This walkthrough is a simplified guide and does not constitute legal or tax advice. Reportability turns on the detailed FATCA definitions, the applicable IGA and your jurisdiction's guidance — always confirm the outcome against the applicable rules or a qualified adviser.
