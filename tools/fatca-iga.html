IGA MODEL FINDER

The US implements FATCA through Intergovernmental Agreements (IGAs). Under a Model 1 IGA, Financial Institutions report to their own tax authority, which exchanges the data with the IRS. Under a Model 2 IGA, Financial Institutions report directly to the IRS. Pick a jurisdiction to see which model applies.

The two models at a glance

Model 1 — report to your authority

FIs identify US Reportable Accounts and report them to their local tax authority, which exchanges the information with the IRS. Most jurisdictions use Model 1. A reciprocal (1A) version has the US share information back; a non-reciprocal (1B) version does not.

Model 2 — report to the IRS

FIs register with and report US accounts directly to the IRS, supplemented by government-to-government exchange of information on request. A smaller group of jurisdictions uses Model 2.

For reference only. IGA status and models change over time — always confirm against the US Treasury's official FATCA resource center and the applicable agreement. This list covers the model type; it does not state exact in-force dates.
