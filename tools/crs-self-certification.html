SELF-CERTIFICATION CHECKER

What this checks

A self-certification is the declaration an account holder (or Controlling Person) gives a Reporting Financial Institution to confirm their jurisdiction(s) of tax residence and Tax Identification Number(s). Under CRS it is mandatory for all new accounts at opening, and the RFI must confirm the certification is reasonable against the information collected during onboarding.
