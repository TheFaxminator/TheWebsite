ENTITY & FI CLASSIFICATION

Where CRS starts

Every CRS obligation flows from one question: is the entity a Financial Institution (FI)? If it is, its category determines its duties, and it is either a Reporting or a Non-Reporting FI. If it is not an FI, it is a Non-Financial Entity (NFE) — which then matters when it holds an account elsewhere. Work through the questions to classify an entity.

Classify an entity
Non-Reporting FI categories

A Financial Institution in one of these categories is exempt from CRS reporting. The precise list — and any conditions — is set by each jurisdiction's implementing legislation.

Governmental Entity, International Organisation or Central Bank — including their wholly owned agencies and instrumentalities
Broad / Narrow Participation Retirement Fund, and Pension Funds of a Governmental Entity, International Organisation or Central Bank
Exempt Collective Investment Vehicle — a CIV whose interests are all held by or through non-reportable persons
Trustee-Documented Trust — the trustee reports on the trust's behalf, so the trust itself does not report
Other low-risk entities defined as Non-Reporting FIs in local law

This walkthrough is a simplified guide and does not constitute legal or tax advice. FI classification turns on the detailed CRS definitions and your jurisdiction's implementing legislation — always confirm the outcome against the applicable rules or a qualified adviser.
