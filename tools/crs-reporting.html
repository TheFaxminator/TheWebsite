REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

What must be reported

For every Reportable Account, the Reporting Financial Institution submits a defined set of information to its competent authority, which then exchanges it with the account holder's jurisdiction(s) of tax residence. The reportable data falls into three parts — who holds the account, the account itself, and the financial activity on it.

01 · Account-Holder Identity

The identity information reported for each Reportable Person depends on whether they are an individual, an entity, or a Controlling Person of a Passive NFE.

Full name
Residential address
Jurisdiction(s) of tax residence
Tax Identification Number (TIN) for each jurisdiction of residence
Date of birth
Full legal name
Registered / principal office address
Jurisdiction(s) of tax residence
Tax Identification Number (TIN) for each jurisdiction of residence

Where the entity is a Passive NFE with one or more Reportable Controlling Persons, report the entity and each such Controlling Person — see the Controlling Person tab.

Full name
Residential address
Jurisdiction(s) of tax residence
Tax Identification Number (TIN) for each jurisdiction of residence
Date of birth

Controlling Person information is reported in addition to the entity account information, not instead of it. Both the entity-level and Controlling Person-level fields go into the return.

02 · Account Details

Reported once for each Reportable Account, regardless of who holds it.

Account number — or functional equivalent where no account number exists
Name and identifying number of the Reporting Financial Institution
Account balance or value as at the end of the calendar year (or other reporting period)
If the account was closed during the year — a record of the closure (in place of the year-end balance)
03 · Financial Information

Report the gross amounts paid or credited to the account during the reporting period, and the currency of each amount. Which of these apply depends on the nature of the account.

Total gross interest
Total gross dividends
Total gross other income
Total gross proceeds from the sale or redemption of financial assets
Total gross amounts otherwise paid or credited to the account holder, including redemption and insurance/annuity payments
Points to watch
Currency. Report the currency in which each amount is denominated — figures are not converted to a single reporting currency.
Reporting period. Balances and values are taken as at year-end (or up to the point of closure); income and proceeds cover the full reporting period.
Nil returns & undocumented accounts. Some jurisdictions require nil reporting and the separate reporting of undocumented accounts — confirm against local rules.
TIN & date of birth. For pre-existing accounts, a TIN and date of birth need not be reported if not in the records and not otherwise required to be collected — reasonable efforts to obtain them apply.

This information is for guidance purposes only and does not constitute legal or tax advice. The precise data elements, formats and thresholds are set by each jurisdiction's implementing legislation and the OECD CRS schema — always confirm against the applicable rules.
