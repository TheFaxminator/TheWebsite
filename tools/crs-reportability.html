ACCOUNT REPORTABILITY

Is the account reportable?

An account is a Reportable Account when it is held by one or more Reportable Persons — persons tax resident in a Reportable Jurisdiction — or, for a Passive NFE, when one or more of its Controlling Persons is a Reportable Person. Choose the account type and work through the questions to reach a determination.
