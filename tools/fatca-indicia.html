US INDICIA CHECKLIST

What triggers a closer look

US indicia are the facts in an account file that suggest the holder may be a US person. Finding one does not by itself make the account reportable — it means the FI must seek documentation to confirm or cure the indicium. If it cannot be cured, the account is treated as held by a US person. The individual indicia below track the FATCA electronic-search list; entity accounts and curing documentation follow.

01 · Indicia by account type

Select the account type to see the US indicia to search for.

US citizenship or residence. Identification of the holder as a US citizen or resident — including a US green card
US place of birth. An unambiguous indication of a place of birth in the United States
US address. A current US mailing or residence address — including a US PO box
US telephone number. One or more current US telephone numbers (if the only telephone number is US, this is a stronger indicium)
Standing instructions. Standing instructions to transfer funds to an account maintained in the United States
Power of attorney / signatory. A currently effective power of attorney or signatory authority granted to a person with a US address
In-care-of / hold-mail address. An "in-care-of" or "hold mail" address that is the sole address on file for the holder

The last two — a US "hold mail / in-care-of" only address, and a US POA/signatory — are treated as weaker indicia under many IGAs and may be cured with less documentation than the first five.

US place of organisation. The entity is organised or incorporated in the United States — it may itself be a Specified US Person
US address. A current US address in the entity's records
Passive NFFE status. The entity is a Passive NFFE — triggering a look-through to its Controlling Persons
US Controlling Persons. One or more Controlling Persons with US indicia (US citizenship/residence, birthplace, address, phone, etc.)
Nonparticipating FFI. The account holder is an FFI that is not compliant — relevant for transitional-period reporting

For an entity, indicia work in two stages: first classify the entity (Specified US Person, FFI, Active or Passive NFFE), then, for a Passive NFFE, apply the individual indicia list to each Controlling Person. Use the Account Reportability tool to walk this through.

02 · Curing US indicia

Where an indicium is found, the account is treated as a US Reportable Account unless the FI obtains and retains documentation that resolves it. Typical curing evidence:

Non-US self-certification. A self-certification (Form W-8 or equivalent) establishing the holder's non-US status
US place of birth. A self-certification of non-US status plus either a Certificate of Loss of Nationality, or a reasonable explanation of why US citizenship was never acquired / was renounced, together with a non-US passport
US address / phone. A self-certification of non-US residence and documentary evidence of non-US status (e.g. a government-issued non-US ID)
Standing instructions. A self-certification and documentary evidence establishing non-US status
POA / hold-mail only. A self-certification, or documentary evidence, establishing the holder's non-US status
03 · Points to watch
Electronic vs paper search. Pre-existing high-value individual accounts also require a paper-record search and a relationship-manager enquiry, not just an electronic search.
Thresholds. IGAs allow certain pre-existing accounts below de-minimis thresholds to be excluded from review — confirm the thresholds that apply.
Change in circumstances. New indicia arising after onboarding trigger a fresh review and, if not cured, reporting.
Recordkeeping. Retain the self-certifications and documentary evidence relied on to cure any indicium.

This checklist is for guidance purposes only and does not constitute legal or tax advice. The precise indicia, thresholds and curing procedures are set by the FATCA regulations and the applicable Intergovernmental Agreement — always confirm against the applicable rules.
